Tudore Tranquility

トゥドレ トランクイリティ

A plant-based fine dining restaurant by chef Mamta Reid, tucked away in a quiet residential neighbourhood of Yoyogi-Uehara. It has won international vegan awards several times, and serves an 8-course menu where vegetables and fruit take centre stage.
